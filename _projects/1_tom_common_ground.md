An in-progress project on the relationship between Theory of Mind, common ground, and pragmatic communication.
We ask whether language models distinguish mutual knowledge from one-sided knowledge, how they adapt when common ground changes,
and how philosophical distinctions about mental-state attribution can be turned into testable computational hypotheses.

_Status: in progress, with collaborators._
